05 // Research

Published Research.

FYP LSTM · FinBERT · Python

M3-FX: A Multi-Modal, Multi-Model Forex Forecasting Framework for the LKR/USD Currency Pair

Accepted — Central Bank of Sri Lanka International Research Conference

Designed a unified LSTM-based forecasting framework integrating four previously siloed signal types: news sentiment (via FinBERT), central bank communication tone (fine-tuned hawkish/dovish classifier), technical indicators, and macroeconomic fundamentals.

  • Achieved a Mean Absolute Error of 0.672 LKR and RMSE of 0.8415, with a Theil’s U statistic of 0.578 confirming the model outperforms a naïve random walk benchmark.
  • Delivered 52.76% directional accuracy and calibrated prediction intervals to 84.7% coverage, supporting practical risk-management applications.
